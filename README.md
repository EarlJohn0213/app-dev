## My Favorite Series: *Black Clover*

**Black Clover** is a fantasy action anime about **Asta**, a boy born without magic in a world where nearly everyone has it. He trains hard to become the **Wizard King**, the strongest mage in the Clover Kingdom.

### Main Characters
- Asta
- Yuno
- Noelle Silva
- Yami Sukehiro
- Luck Voltia

### Magic Knights Squads
1. Black Bulls
2. Golden Dawn
3. Crimson Lion Kings
4. Silver Eagles

### Why I Like It
- Asta never gives up, even without magic
- Exciting fights and powerful **anti-magic** abilities
- Strong themes of friendship and hard work

### Quote
> Asta's dream: to surpass everyone and become the Wizard King.
